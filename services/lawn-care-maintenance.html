What Lawn Care and Maintenance Involves

Regular visits cover mowing at the right height for the season, edging along walkways and beds so the lawn has a clean line instead of a ragged one, and fertilization timed to actually help the grass instead of just checking a box. We also handle seasonal cleanup, clearing fallen leaves, cutting back spent growth, and tidying planting beds so the yard doesn't slide into overgrown territory between visits.

For properties with basic trees and shrubs, we include light trimming to keep growth in check, so nothing outgrows its space or starts crowding walkways and windows.

When You Need Lawn Care

Regular maintenance makes sense any time you'd rather spend your weekends doing something other than yard work, but there are clearer signals too: grass that's grown noticeably shaggy between your own mowing attempts, beds that have gone weedy, a lawn that's started thinning or yellowing without an obvious cause, or a yard you simply haven't had time to keep up with since a move, a busy season at work, or an injury. A recurring plan catches small issues, like early weed growth or a dry patch, before they turn into bigger problems.

Why Yards Get Away From Homeowners

Grass and plants don't wait for a free weekend. A single missed mowing during a fast growth period in spring can leave a lawn looking unkempt for weeks, and weeds establish fast in beds that go even a few weeks without attention. Our hot, dry summers add another layer: lawns need more frequent care to stay healthy through the heat, which is exactly when most people have the least time or energy for yard work.

A consistent schedule keeps all of this from compounding, so you're never facing the kind of overgrown yard that takes a full day to bring back under control.

What Affects the Cost

Maintenance pricing depends on a few factors:

  • The size of your property and how much lawn versus planting bed it includes
  • How often you want visits, weekly, biweekly, or monthly
  • Whether you want fertilization and seasonal cleanup included or handled separately
  • Any basic tree and shrub trimming included in the visit

We'll put together a plan and a price based on your actual yard, not a flat rate that doesn't reflect how much work your property really needs.

One-Time Cleanup vs. Ongoing Maintenance

If your yard has gotten away from you and just needs to be brought back under control, a one-time cleanup handles the overgrowth, debris, and any overdue trimming in a single visit. Ongoing maintenance is the better fit if you want the yard to stay at that level going forward without another cleanup down the road. Many homeowners start with a one-time cleanup to reset the yard, then move to a recurring schedule so it never gets back to that point again.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should my lawn be mowed?

Most lawns do best with weekly mowing during the growing season and can stretch to biweekly during cooler, slower-growth months. We'll recommend a schedule based on your specific grass type and how the lawn responds through the season.

Do you offer one-time cleanups or only recurring service?

Both. A one-time cleanup is a great option if your yard needs to be brought back under control before starting a regular schedule, or if you just need it handled once for an event or a sale. We're happy to do either.

Can you maintain a drought-tolerant or turf yard too?

Yes. Maintenance for a drought-tolerant landscape looks different from a traditional lawn, more focused on pruning, weeding, and mulch upkeep than mowing, and artificial turf mainly needs occasional rinsing and debris removal. We adjust the visit to match what your yard actually needs.

What's included in seasonal cleanup?

Seasonal cleanup typically covers clearing fallen leaves and debris, cutting back spent perennials and overgrown shrubs, refreshing mulch where needed, and general bed tidying. It's a heavier visit than a routine mowing stop, meant to reset the yard for the season ahead.

Can I pause or adjust my maintenance schedule?

Yes, we can adjust visit frequency seasonally or pause service if you're traveling or your needs change. Just let us know and we'll update your schedule accordingly.
